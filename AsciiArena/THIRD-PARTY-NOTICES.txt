ASCII Arena prototype

The ASCII character patterns, arena and effects are project-authored assets.
The colored hero sprites and animation poses were generated with image_gen.
Spark is an original character. Pac-Man and Doomguy are fan prototype guests;
their character identities belong to their respective rights holders. These
are not licensed commercial character assets. See docs/assets.md and the
provenance records in art/sprites/hero-animations for their creation history.
The current world/UI glyph atlas uses JetBrains Mono (see below); the previous
bitmap atlas was drawn for this project. The project has not yet selected
a general source-code license.
