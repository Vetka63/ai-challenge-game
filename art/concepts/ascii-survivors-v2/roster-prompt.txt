Asset type: ONE character art-direction board for an original 2D ASCII survivor-like game, landscape 1536x1024. This is a concept sheet, not a screenshot of a working game.

Medium: genuine visual ASCII art. Every body and prop must visibly consist of legible monospace typewriter glyphs on a coarse fixed grid: / backslash | _ - ^ v o O x + * ( ) [ ] < > : . =. Crisp separate glyphs and large black negative spaces between limbs. An outlined hood, two eyes, robe, feet, weapons made from these glyphs. Never use a lone @ as a character. Do not fill silhouettes with dense # textures. No painterly pixel art or solid illustration contours, no detailed texture, no grassy scenery. Tiny restrained halo only, hard readable glyph cores, NO fuzzy bloom.
Composition: tasteful spacious 3 by 3 roster board on uniform near-black #080b12, nine separated cells, consistent centered baselines, thin extremely dark dividers, small understated mono labels above each character. Large enough that individual text glyphs are obvious, not hundreds of tiny dots. Characters formed from roughly 9-17 glyph columns and 10-17 rows. Show front/three-quarter game-facing full bodies with distinct head, torso, arms, feet. Sprite-like flat camera; not portraits, no 3D.
