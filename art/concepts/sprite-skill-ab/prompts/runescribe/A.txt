Design an original readable character for a dark 2D arena survivor-like inspired by the legibility of ASCII Survivors. The visual language is colourful terminal-glyph art: a compact recognizable creature assembled from a few bold angular punctuation-like strokes, brackets, slashes, short bars and square dots, with deliberate empty gaps. Preserve the feeling of a small composed ASCII creature; avoid a detailed conventional fantasy illustration or a lone @ symbol. Crisp flat luminous-coloured marks, simple silhouette, minimal internal detail, no bloom or gradients. Front-facing neutral pose, orthographic game camera with a slight view of the top, full body and any held equipment visible. One isolated character on genuine transparent background, square canvas, centred with clear margins. No captions, words, logos, watermark, floor, scenery or extra creatures. Readability on near-black at small gameplay size is the priority.

Character: A small humanoid mage with a triangular cyan hood, a dark empty face opening with two ivory square eyes, a short split cyan robe, two separately visible feet and a slender ivory staff held to the viewer's right. A small gold four-point star crowns the staff. The hood, eyes, divided hem and staff are the four identity cues. Cyan and ivory dominate; gold is a tiny accent.

Intended gameplay footprint: about 48 by 56 screen pixels. This is a concept for that scale, not a request for literal output dimensions.
